Our Story

Where Every Soul Finds Home

Welcome to Tabernacle of Grace City of Hope Church — a vibrant community of faith rooted in the heart of Nairobi, Kenya. We are a people transformed by grace, sustained by hope, and united in worship.

Whether you are a lifelong believer or simply seeking something greater, our doors are open wide. Here you will find not just a church, but a family — a place where you belong.

We believe that every person carries a divine purpose, and our mission is to help you discover, develop, and deploy yours for the glory of God and the good of our community.

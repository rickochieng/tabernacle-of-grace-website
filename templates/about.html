Our Story

About Our Church

Founded in faith, sustained by grace, and called to transform Nairobi and beyond.

Our History

Tabernacle of Grace City of Hope Church was founded in Nairobi, Kenya, with a simple but burning vision: to create a community where hurting people could encounter the healing grace of God. Beginning as a small prayer group meeting in a home on Wambiri Road, the church grew steadily as God added souls and provided provision.

Located opposite Lenana School Primary, our church sits at the heart of the community we serve. From those early gatherings, we have grown into a family of hundreds, united by love, led by the Spirit, and anchored in the Word of God.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To bring hope, grace, healing, and spiritual transformation to our community through the Gospel of Jesus Christ — touching lives from Nairobi to the nations.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of hope in every neighbourhood — a church where every person is known, loved, equipped, and sent to fulfil their God-given purpose.

What We Believe

We are a Bible-believing, Spirit-filled Christian church. We hold to the foundational truths of Scripture: the Trinity, the virgin birth and resurrection of Jesus Christ, salvation by grace through faith alone, the authority of the Holy Bible, the power of the Holy Spirit, and the imminent return of Jesus Christ.
